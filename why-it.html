My motivation:

As all little kids do, I used to ask LOTS of questions mostly without thinking beforehand. Fortunately my parents always pushed me to find the solutions myself rather than just feeding me with answers. This continued all throughout my adolescence and now I am kind of used to just carry around a small notebook with me and plot down different problems I may encouter, solving them myself.

Is this efficient? Not really, but I like to think that it keeps me sharp.

For this reason, I found ICT one of the most suitable fields for me. Any problem has a multitude of solutions and it is up to you to find the one that is a perfect balance between efficiency and readability (always code for the maintainer).

Long-term I would like to get a job that offers me enough financial freedom, but at the same time is also challanging, allowing me to pick up how other solve problems (currently aiming for a job at one of the FAANG companies as a Software engineer) but, at the same time start a lot of personal projects fixing/improving aspect of societies' everyday life.
(Current objective: create a compatibilty layer between Google Maps and fietsrouteplanner.eu, since Google bike routes are inaccurate and fietsrouteplanner.eu is inconvenient to use, but I still need to learn a few things).

The reason why I specifically chose HZ is also quite simple. I knew that I wanted to study in the Netherlands, and as part of my research I participated in the International University Fair where, alongside other universities, HZ was present with a teacher who told me a lot about how things are done at HZ, the curriculum, the different tracks, and how and what I can become after graduation. The decision became final after I attended the open days in fall of 2025 and had a really cool talk with Hugo.
